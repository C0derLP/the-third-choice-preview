About Niyia

Niyia Whitsett helps people recognize what is affecting how they heal, function, and move forward and turns those insights into practical tools they can use in everyday life.She is a nonclinical Health Educator, entrepreneur, published author, speaker, and visionary curator whose work brings together emotional well-being, restorative practices, behavior, and real-world decision-making. At the center of her work is one defining question: Can people actually use it?

For more than 15 years, that question has shaped how Niyia studies, creates, and serves. She has developed health-education programs, botanical products, organizational training systems, and restorative experiences that address stress, emotional barriers, personal restoration, and the everyday conditions that influence how people live and perform. Her ability is not simply identifying what people need. It is translating complex or deeply personal challenges into experiences, language, and systems that help people understand what may be blocking them and what they can do next. Niyia’s multidisciplinary background includes specialized training through the HEAL Center and the Grief Recovery Institute, along with certification-based study in restorative wellness practices. She studied psychology at Clark Atlanta University and Cuyahoga Community College and is the recipient of an Honorary Doctorate in Humanitarianism from Enoch Grove Christian University. She further strengthened her business leadership through Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses, developed in partnership with Babson College. Her studies are strengthened by lived experience, discernment, and the God-given spiritual gifts that allow her to see both the person and the system while assisting them with practical structures that help them move forward.

Niyia is also the visionary founder of United Women in White, a movement rooted in connection, advocacy, philanthropy, and one guiding conviction: Embrace other woman you don’t know what it takes to be her. She is the author of Mind Your Style and Heal Thy Self and the creator of numerous thought provoking projects. Her work has been featured on national television, and she has shared platforms with globally recognized thought leaders.Niyia loves seeded fruit, cultivating plants, traveling, personal style, substance, and conversations deep enough to shift the way people see themselves. She is living proof that a woman can be deeply committed to her healing, wholeness, and purpose without losing her joy, beauty, individuality, or edge. Whether she is developing a program, designing a product, speaking from a stage, or gathering women around a table, Niyia’s mission remains constant: to reveal what may be standing between people and a brighter tomorrow.
