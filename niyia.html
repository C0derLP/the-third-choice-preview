About Niyia

Niyia Whitsett is the Founder & CEO of Self-Approved Wellness Systems, a nonclinical Health Educator, author, speaker, program developer, and consultant whose work focuses on helping people and organizations translate health education into practical, usable support for everyday life. For more than 15 years, Niyia has studied and worked across alternative health education, emotional well-being, behavior, restorative practices, and community-based programming. Her specialized training includes aromatherapy through the HEAL Center in Atlanta, Reiki, Grief Recovery through the Grief Recovery Institute, and restorative wellness practices. She has also studied psychology at Clark Atlanta University and Cuyahoga Community College. Her experience extends beyond individual wellness. Niyia has developed and coordinated programs for behavioral health agencies, after-school initiatives, women, families, community organizations, and professional teams. Her work includes health-education programming, stress-management education, professional development, curriculum design, botanical product development, group facilitation, and the creation of practical systems that help people better understand the habits, stressors, behaviors, and life conditions affecting how they function. At the center of her work is a simple question: Can people actually use what they are learning? That question has shaped the way she approaches everything from individual consultations to organizational programming. Rather than giving people more information to remember, Niyia focuses on helping them understand what may be getting in the way, what can realistically change, and what tools can support them in taking the next step. Her business leadership was further strengthened through Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses, with curriculum developed in partnership with Babson College. She is also the recipient of an Honorary Doctorate in Humanitarianism from Enoch Grove Christian University. Niyia is the author of Mind Your Style and Heal Thy Self and the visionary founder of United Women in White, a women-centered movement rooted in connection, advocacy, philanthropy, and shared experience. Her work has been featured on national television, and she has appeared on panels and platforms alongside nationally and internationally recognized speakers and thought leaders. Faith is also an important part of Niyia’s life and leadership. She identifies discernment, service, and her God-given spiritual gifts as part of how she understands her calling and responsibility to others. Today, Niyia’s work spans three primary areas: nonclinical health education, wellness products, and program design and consulting. Individuals utilize her services for practical health education and wellness support, while organizations engage her to develop programs, curriculum, professional development, and community-based initiatives. Her work is grounded in one belief: Knowing better is not enough. People need practical ways to live better. This version is much stronger for the audience you described because a professor can see your educational foundation, a doctor can immediately see that your work is nonclinical and within a defined lane, a business leader can see what they can contract you for, and someone off the street can understand what you do without needing to know wellness terminology. Most importantly, it finally answers “Why would I hire Niyia?”: you take information, behavior, stress, and real-life challenges and turn them into usable products, education, and programs.
