Questions worth asking

Leave seeing differently.

What is The Third Choice™? +

The Third Choice™ is a live thought forum designed for women who are no longer satisfied with choosing between success and themselves. Through conversation, evidence, lived experience, and guided reflection, we examine the hidden assumptions that shape our decisions and discover the options we couldn't see before.

Why is it called The Third Choice™? +

Most of life's biggest decisions are presented as two opposing options: career or family, ambition or peace, logic or intuition, wealth or purpose. The Third Choice™ begins with a different question: What if those aren't the only choices available?

Who belongs in the room? +

Women who carry significant responsibility for businesses, families, organizations, or communities and who value thoughtful conversation as much as measurable results.

What actually happens during the day? +

A carefully designed sequence of conversations, research, reflection, and practical frameworks challenges conventional thinking and expands how participants approach leadership, relationships, health, wealth, and identity.

What makes this different? +

Most events offer answers. The Third Choice™ teaches participants how to ask better questions— because better questions produce better decisions, and better decisions change lives.

Will there be actionable takeaways? +

Absolutely. Every insight is paired with practical application, including mental frameworks, decision-making tools, and reflection practices.
