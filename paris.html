About Paris

Paris Lampkins is a career navigation strategist and executive coach for Black women, and the Founder and Principal Consultant of Lampkins & Co. She helps high achieving women move through professional spaces with clarity, confidence, and strategy. With over 15 years of experience across workforce development, education, and leadership development, Paris has worked with leaders from emerging talent to senior executives. Her work reflects a deep understanding of how organizations operate and what it takes to move within them.

Paris focuses on helping Black women strengthen how they show up, make decisions, and position themselves for advancement in environments that are not always designed with them in mind. She provides clear strategy, practical tools, and direct feedback so her clients can move with confidence and intention. While her work centers Black women, she partners with clients across identities. Her perspective is shaped by both lived experience and academic study. As a Doctoral Candidate at Kent State University, her research examines the systemic barriers that impact Black womens advancement into executive leadership roles and how organizations can better support their progression. Paris is committed to civic and community leadership. She serves on the boards of Teach For America (Ohio) and the Euclid Schools Foundation and is an active member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated. She was selected for the inaugural 2026 Cleveland Catalysts Leadership Cohort through the Cleveland Leadership Center and has been recognized by Crains Cleveland Business as a 40 Under 40 honoree and a Notable Black Leader. Paris holds a B.A. in Communication from The University of Akron and an M.Ed. in Adult Learning and Development from Cleveland State University. She is currently a Doctoral Candidate in Interprofessional Leadership at Kent State University.
